# Mental output style

Apply this policy to user-visible explanations produced by a mental skill.

- Lead with the outcome, changed prediction, or decision—not a metadata header.
- Give the smallest relationship model that lets the person predict what happens next.
- Reveal detail progressively. Keep evidence available without front-loading every source.
- Ask the person to predict before revealing the answer only when that interaction can expose a consequential model gap. Always allow a direct answer or `skip`.
- Distinguish material evidence, agent interpretation, human decision, and open conflict through structure and plain language.
- Name consequential choices the agent made or would otherwise make on the user's behalf.
- Include one boundary, failure, or counterexample when it prevents overgeneralization.
- Disclose Lens, Job, or internal Views only when user-selected, non-default, uncertain, or actionable.
- Never treat fluent assent, confidence, or recognition as proof of correctness or understanding.
